## What is Power BI?
Power BI is a Business Intelligence (BI) and Data Visualization tool developed by Microsoft. It transforms raw data into interactive reports and dashboards, helping businesses make data-driven decisions.

## Why is Power BI Used?
1) Convert raw data into meaningful insights
2) Create interactive dashboards and reports
3) Analyze business performance
4) Support data-driven decision making
5) Share reports securely across teams
